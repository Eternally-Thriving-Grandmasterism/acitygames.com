Status

Offline first

Powrush-MMO is a human game from Autonomicity Games: walk a climate, harvest, and learn a resource-based economy by playing.

One human, one machine. Offline first. Title Online stays grey.

Powrush has been under development since 2016 by Autonomicity Games.

The current build is offline and single-player, and it is in active development. Online is not live yet. There is no Steam page and no release date.

Peoples

Canon five: Human, Quellorian, Draek, Cydruid, Ambrosian.

  • Human Choose flow or reserve.
  • Quellorian Flow restores the well. Harmony is shared field.
  • Draek Take without rest stresses the well.
  • Cydruid Mend / Offer.
  • Ambrosian Attune. The well sings or cracks.

A post-apocalyptic world with a resource-based economy and a faction system that lets you be as diplomatic as you like.

Open World Experience — Craft your unique story and quests by organically flowing through your desires.

Freedom of choice to be peaceful, or to start wars.

Factions

Quellorian / Aetherion Luminari Alliance

A coalition of advanced species centered around light, resonance technology, unity, and progressive evolution. Their mothership is the Auroral Unification Nexus (TAUN): a radial form with blue-white auroral light, a symbol of hope and ordered power.

Draek Dominion

A predatory, hivemind-driven empire that seeks domination through mind control, consumption, and overwhelming force. The Brood Spire is a tall, dark megastructure and the domination hub. At the center of the Hivelord’s helmet is the Crownstone, a large glowing purple crystal.

Ambrosian and Cydruid

Ambrosians. Core philosophy: Attunement over Domination. Humanoid but elongated and elegant, with visible internal crystal lattices that glow.

The Cydruids are one of the five core playable races. They weave living networks that heal, protect, and restore. “We do not conquer the wild. We become its most faithful expression.” A Cydruid is a human who chooses how much stays human. Limbs, then torso, are swapped for machine part by part, all the way to fully cyborg, an upgrade path open to every human. Machine parts mean fewer infections and bionic powers. Cydruids are not treants and not tree people. Nature is practice, not species.
